# Research agenda

## Central question
**Under what combinations of ambient exposure and cooling capacity does a simplified cold-chain system cross a specified temperature threshold?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Excursion time increases nonlinearly with ambient severity and insufficient cooling.

### H2
Small cooling improvements near the stability boundary can materially reduce excursions.

### H3
A simple interpretable thermal model is a useful baseline before learned forecasting.

## Experiments
1. Grid-sweep ambient temperature and cooling power; map excursion regions.
2. Introduce changing ambient profiles representing loading, transit, and unloading.
3. Compare fixed cooling with a future feedback controller under equal energy budgets.

## Metrics
- max temperature
- excursion hours
- time to threshold
- cooling energy proxy
- trajectory RMSE after future calibration
